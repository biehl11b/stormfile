# StormFile

A phone app that helps Gulf Coast homeowners, landlords and small businesses understand their insurance and get claim-ready before a hurricane.

- Plain-English policy summary from the declarations page
- "Do I have enough?" rebuild and inflation checks
- Room-by-room photos, receipts, upgrades, vehicles
- Storm alert levels and checklists
- Claim tracker with evacuation and living-expense receipts

Test version. All information people enter is saved only on their own phone.
