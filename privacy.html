StormFile Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026 · StormFile is made by Coastline Code LLC, Mississippi

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use StormFile, the app made by Coastline Code LLC ("we," "us"). The short version: your policy, photos, receipts and claim records stay on your phone. We don't collect them, see them, sell them or share them.

1. What we don't collect. StormFile doesn't require an account. We don't collect or receive your name, email, phone number or address; your insurance policy details; anything you type into StormFile, including properties, rooms, items, claim notes, call logs, letters and expenses; your photos, receipts or documents; or your location. StormFile has no ads, no ad tracking, no analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites.

2. Where your information is stored. Everything you save in StormFile is stored on your device, inside the app's own storage. Your claim packet PDF, calendar reminders and letters are made on your phone. On iPhone, if your phone backs up to iCloud, your iPhone's backup settings control whether StormFile's data is included; those backups are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy, and we can't see them. StormFile also lets you make your own locked backup file (Settings → Backup), encrypted with a passphrase only you know. Because we never receive your information, we can't recover it if you lose your phone or delete the app, so keep a backup somewhere other than your phone.

3. Phone permissions.

  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose to take or add a photo or receipt. Photos are saved inside StormFile on your phone.
  • Location: if you allow it, StormFile adds where a photo was taken to that photo's record, so you can show it was taken at your property. The location is saved only with the photo on your phone. You can say no and StormFile still works.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint: if you turn on the lock, your phone checks your face or fingerprint. StormFile never sees or stores it.

4. Information you choose to share.

  • Claim packet, letters and backups: when you share a PDF, letter or backup file, you decide who gets it and how it's sent. After that, it's handled by the app you used, such as email, text messages or a cloud drive.
  • Calendar reminders: when you add renewal, follow-up or storm season reminders, they go into your own calendar app.
  • Phone numbers and websites: when you tap a phone number or website on the Help and resources screen, your phone opens it. That agency or company's own privacy policy applies to anything you share with them.
  • Emailing us: if you send feedback or contact us, we receive what you send, such as your email address and message. We use it only to answer you and to improve StormFile. Please don't send us your policy number, claim number or other private details.

5. The web version. StormFile can also be used in a web browser. The web version's files come from our web host, which, like any website, can see your device's internet (IP) address and may keep it briefly in standard server logs. We don't use it to identify you. Your records are stored in your browser on your device and are never sent to the website.

6. App stores. Apple and Google may collect information when you buy, download or use apps from their stores, such as payment details, crash reports or download counts, under their own privacy policies. We never receive your payment details. Any reports they share with us are summaries that don't identify you.

7. Children. StormFile is made for adults managing their homes, rentals and businesses. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

8. Deleting your information. You can delete any entry in the app, erase everything in Settings → Erase everything, or delete the app. Since we don't have your information, there's nothing for us to delete on our end. If you emailed us and want that email deleted, ask us at the address below.

9. Security. Your information is protected by your phone's own security, such as your passcode, Face ID or fingerprint, and by StormFile's own lock if you turn it on. Backups are encrypted with your passphrase. Keep your phone locked and your software up to date.

10. Changes to this policy. If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. If we add a feature that sends any of your information off your phone, such as reading your policy with AI or online backup, we'll tell you in the app and ask before it's turned on.
