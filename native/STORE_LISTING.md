## Full description: Apple App Store version (4,000 max)

Mentions only iPhone features. Apple rejects listings that mention other phones or stores (guideline 2.3.10).

StormFile gets your home, rental or business ready for an insurance claim before the storm, and keeps the claim organized after it.

Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.

KNOW WHAT YOU'RE COVERED FOR
• Enter your declarations page once and see each coverage explained in plain English
• Your hurricane or wind deductible worked out in dollars, not just a percentage
• A building-cost check that adds recent inflation, so you can spot a gap before a storm does
• Flood and storm surge gaps called out (most homeowners policies don't cover flood)
• Renewal reminders in your calendar 45 days ahead, so there's time to shop or adjust

DOCUMENT EVERYTHING BEFORE THE STORM
• Room-by-room photos with the date taken, and where taken if you allow location
• Items with what you paid and the year you bought them
• Receipts and home upgrades (roof, windows, HVAC) attached to each item
• Vehicles, campers and outbuildings
• Lock your before-storm record when you file, so anything added later is clearly marked

STORM AND FIRE SEASONS FOR YOUR STATE
• Hurricane, wildfire, grass fire, tornado and winter storm seasons for all 50 states and DC
• A get-ready checklist for each season
• Yearly reminders added to your iPhone's Calendar 3 weeks before each season starts

ORGANIZE THE CLAIM
• Claim number, adjuster, and a dated log of every call and email
• Claim deadlines worked out for you: the time limits Texas, Florida, Louisiana and Alabama set for insurers, plus the flood insurance proof-of-loss deadline, added to your calendar
• Evacuation and living-expense receipts (ALE) with running totals
• Ready-to-send letters: follow-up, request for the estimate, status, living expenses, supplement, policy request, and a complaint to your state insurance department built from your call log
• One claim packet PDF with your policy summary, photos, contents list, receipts and call log, made on your phone and ready to send to your adjuster with a note already written

HELP IN YOUR STATE
• Your state insurance department with tap-to-call and its complaint page, for when a claim stalls
• Your state emergency management agency, FEMA, SBA disaster loans and flood insurance (NFIP) contacts
• Gulf Coast wind pools and fortified-roof grant programs where they exist

MORE THAN ONE PLACE
• Homes, rentals and businesses in one app, each with its own policy or sharing one

SHARE WITH YOUR HOUSEHOLD
• Give a spouse or co-owner their own copy, phone to phone, unlocked with a QR code. No account and nothing uploaded

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Lock with a PIN and Face ID or Touch ID
• Locked backups you control (encrypted with your passphrase): save one to iCloud Drive, or use it to move to a new iPhone

EN ESPAÑOL Y ACCESIBLE
• The whole app in English or Spanish (letters and the claim packet stay in English for your insurer)
• Works with VoiceOver, follows your iPhone's text size (Larger Text), and respects Reduce Motion

NOT A GOVERNMENT APP
StormFile isn't made by, connected to or approved by any government agency. Government contacts and rules in the app come from official sources, each linked in the app: state insurance departments and emergency management agencies (their official .gov websites), FEMA (fema.gov, disasterassistance.gov, ready.gov), SBA disaster loans (sba.gov), the National Flood Insurance Program (floodsmart.gov), the National Hurricane Center (hurricanes.gov), and state laws on claim deadlines (official state legislature websites).

StormFile organizes your records and explains your policy in plain English. It doesn't sell insurance, give insurance or legal advice, or decide what your policy covers. Confirm coverage with your agent or insurance company. Always follow official weather alerts and evacuation orders.

Made in Pass Christian, Mississippi, by Coastline Code LLC, a veteran-owned company.

## Age check law (Texas now; Utah, Louisiana and California in 2027)

Texas's App Store Accountability Act has been enforceable since June 4, 2026; Utah (May 6, 2027), Louisiana (July 1, 2027) and California (Jan. 1, 2027) follow. Apps must use the stores' age APIs. Done in the code: on each launch the phone app asks Apple (Declared Age Range) or Google (Play Age Signals) whether an age check is required for this person. Only if it is, it asks for the age range: 18+ continues; under 18 sees "StormFile is for adults"; not shared sees "Confirm you're 18 or older" with a button to share. Only a yes/no stays on the phone. If the check can't run (offline, older phone), StormFile stays usable so records are never locked away in a storm. StormFile has no in-app purchases, so there's nothing to rate or approve separately. Apple: the bundle ID needs the **Declared Age Range** capability (Xcode turns it on automatically when it signs the build with the App Store Connect key; if the upload fails on this, tick "Declared Age Range" for com.coastlinecode.stormfile in Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles → Identifiers).

## Apple: Notes for the reviewer (App Review Information)

StormFile needs no login. On first open, tap "I agree — look around with sample data first" to see a sample home with a policy, rooms and a claim, or "I agree — set up my home" to start with your own.
To try the main features with the sample: Policy tab (coverage explained, deductible in dollars, renewal reminder, "What's this?" links that explain insurance words); Rooms tab (take or choose a photo, add an item); Storm tab (seasons for the property's state, "Add yearly reminders to my calendar"); Claim tab (the "Next step" box, then tap a section to open it: claim deadlines, call log, letters, and "Make the claim packet (PDF)"); the "Help and resources" card on the Claim tab (state insurance department and emergency contacts). Settings (top right) → tap "Lock" to set a PIN and Face ID; tap "Backup" to make an encrypted backup file through the share sheet. Settings → Language switches between English and Spanish. The Privacy Policy and Terms links are in Settings → About.
Age check: StormFile asks for the age range only where state law requires it (Texas). Under 18 can't use the app (it's for adults managing their homes and businesses).
All data is stored only on the device; there is no account or server.
Features that use the phone itself: camera and photo library for room and receipt photos, location (optional) to record where a photo was taken, Face ID / passcode lock, the system "New Event" calendar screen and calendar write access for reminders, and the share sheet for letters, PDFs and encrypted backups.
StormFile is not an insurance company, agent or adjuster and doesn't sell insurance. Demo account: not needed.

## Google Play: Permissions

Camera (room, item and receipt photos), location (optional, to note where a photo was taken; saved only with the photo on the phone), fingerprint or face unlock (optional lock), calendar (add the renewal, follow-up and season reminders the user chooses; Android's calendar access also covers seeing the calendar list, which StormFile uses only to find the main calendar). No contacts, microphone, notifications, photo library or storage permissions (photos are chosen through Android's own camera and file chooser, which need no permission). Precise location: Google requires a Play Console declaration for ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION from January 27, 2027. Answer: StormFile records where each room photo was taken so the owner can show it was taken at the insured property; approximate location (about 1-2 miles) can't show that. It's asked only when the user adds a photo, is optional, and stays on the phone.
